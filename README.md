## 🧰 What you need

- An **n8n instance** (n8n Cloud, or self-hosted — everything runs on the free/self-hosted tier)
- A **Google account** — Sheets, Gmail and Drive do the heavy lifting in most templates; no paid CRM needed
- Some templates also use an **AI API key** (pay-as-you-go; the AI steps cost a fraction of a cent per run), the official **WhatsApp Business Platform** (#7), or a **vector database** you own (#10) — each template's guide lists exactly what it needs before you start

Every credential is connected by you, in your own accounts. Nothing here can see, store or send your data anywhere except where you point it.
